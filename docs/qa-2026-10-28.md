## 3. Practice

Every practice step of every lesson was run in headless Chromium, for all 8 project templates. Each run builds the srcdoc the app builds (`buildSrcdoc` over the rendered files), loads it into a `sandbox="allow-scripts"` iframe under the app's own CSP, and reads the posted checks, script errors and console. The runner is `.qa/practice-run.mjs`, which is not committed.

- **Totals.** 152 steps × 8 templates = 1216 runs, with the solution and the starter in each.
- **Broken in the real browser, though jsdom passed: 1 step.** In 05/08 the `search-hidden` check read the `hidden` attribute of `form#search`. The lesson's own script removes that attribute before the checks run, so the reference solution failed for all 8 templates. The check now sees the form. The task says the no-script state is checked by hand, as the content-plan README prescribes for what a check cannot see.
- **After the fix.** Every solution passes and every starter fails, in all 1216 runs. No script error and no page error.
- **Console.** Seven steps log a CSP refusal because their page deliberately names an outside resource and the preview has no network, by design:
  - the starters of 14/07 (the Metrika snippet), 15/06 (reCAPTCHA), 16/04 (a stock image) and 16/07 (a font), all of which the learner removes;
  - 09/03, whose lesson is rewriting `http://` to `https://`, in both starter and solution;
  - the solution of 14/02, which is the real Metrika snippet.

  These are the sandbox doing its job, not defects.

## 6. What is left

- **Legal.** The six «Расхождения между уроками» and the rest of `docs/legal-review.md` are for the lawyer. Nothing legal was resolved here.
- **CSP noise in the preview.** On 79 lessons, the sandbox could stop relative URLs from being requested, which would quiet the console without changing the attributes the checks read. A `<base>` does not work, because the app's `base-uri 'self'` is inherited by the srcdoc. This needs a design in `src/lib/practice/sandbox.ts`, not a content edit.
- **Cross-references.** About 350 places refer to another lesson in words. About seventy became links in this pass, where a lesson leans on an earlier one. The rest are mostly references within a part («в прошлом уроке») or to whole parts, and read fine as they are. Linking them is a mechanical follow-up.
- **Repetition.** About twenty pairs of lessons were compared by section: the ones where concept titles, bold terms or text suggested overlap. A cover-to-cover human read may still find shorter repeats elsewhere.
- **Load.** Under load, axe took over two minutes on three heavy lessons (02/02, 03/06, 08/05) and the sweep timed out on them. Run alone, they are clean.
- **`m3.material.io`.** It answers the runners but not this machine. If Links ever names it, the replacement is Material's GitHub or Wikipedia.
- **«ё» beyond the safe dictionary.** The test catches only the safe list. A new lesson's «все/всё», «узнает/узнаёт» and the like still need a reader.
